Where to Try the Best Tiramisu in Rome

Pompi

I dont think anyone could argue with me when I say tiramisu is the best of the best when it comes to Italian desserts. This layered specialty, which regularly features on my Rome food tours, is made of coffee-drenched ladyfingers, mascarpone cream, cocoa powder, and sometimes a dash of liqueur. Its a sweet treat that many Romans enjoy after a meal.

Tiramisu is a light and rich recipe that hails from the north of Italy in Veneto, but it has become a firm favorite on many dessert menus in Rome and other parts of the country. A perfect serving of tiramisu should be made fresh and balanced without being too sweet..

For anyone whos just landed and wondering where to eat tiramisu in Rome, check out Pompi near Piazza di Spagna. Between the mix of options like strawberry and pistachio and big portions, there are so many reasons why I keep coming back to this small counter with a handful of seats for more.
