Where to Try the Best bistecca alla fiorentina in Florence

Fiaschetteria Trattoria

Known as the most sacred dish in Italian cuisine, Florentine-style beef steak is prepared exclusively with dry-aged beef from Chianina cattle, highly prized for its tenderness. Although bistecca alla Fiorentina is often described as a T-bone steak, it's actually more like a porterhouse—because it's cut closer to the center, the tenderloin is much larger than a typical T-bone.

In fact, bistecca alla Fiorentina is never served medium or well-done, as any true Tuscan will vouch that this dream steak should only be eaten sanguinoso, meaning raw. To enjoy your Florentine-style steak the Italian way, simplicity is key—bring out the rich flavors of the roast beef with just a touch of extra virgin olive oil, rosemary, and salt.

This small restaurant is located just behind the San Lorenzo Central Market, and offers the most delicious and affordable 'bistecca alla Fiorentina' or T-bone steak, always served medium rare.
